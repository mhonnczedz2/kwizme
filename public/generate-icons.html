💡 Tips for Great Icons

  • Text mode: Customize text, colors, and backgrounds to match your brand
  • Upload mode: Use your own logo - images are automatically resized and centered
  • Keep it simple: Icons are displayed at small sizes, so avoid complex details
  • High contrast: Use colors that stand out on both light and dark backgrounds
  • Test it: Check how your icon looks on your device's home screen
