# QuizMe: Executive Summary

**AI-Powered Quiz Generator - Personal Study Tool**

---

## Core Goal

Build a minimal viable product to validate the core hypothesis: *"Students want an AI tool that automatically generates practice quizzes from their PDF study materials."*

## Timeline & Budget

- **Timeline**: 6-8 weeks
- **Budget**: $0-50 initial setup + $10-50/month operating costs
- **Success Metric**: 100 users complete at least one quiz, 40%+ return for a second quiz

---

## Value Proposition

*"Upload your textbook PDF, get a personalized practice quiz in 60 seconds. Study smarter, not harder."*

## Key Differentiators

- **Speed**: Quiz ready in 60 seconds
- **Privacy**: All data stored locally on user's device
- **Simplicity**: No accounts, no login, no complexity
- **Cost**: Free for MVP phase, ultra-low operating costs

---

## Target Market

**Primary**: College students (age 18-24), STEM majors
**Secondary**: High school students, professionals studying for certifications

**Market Size**:
- 20M college students in US
- Addressable: ~5M active studiers who use digital materials
- Serviceable: ~500K early adopters who try new study tools

---

## Competitive Landscape

**Existing Solutions**:
- Quizlet (manual card creation, time-consuming)
- Anki (steep learning curve, no PDF support)
- ChatGPT (requires prompt engineering, no structure)

**Our Advantage**:
- One-click PDF → quiz transformation
- Structured learning experience
- No manual work required

---

## Business Model (Post-Validation)

**Freemium**:
- Free: 5 quizzes/month
- Premium: $4.99/month for unlimited quizzes

**Unit Economics** (projected):
- CAC: $2 (organic/viral)
- LTV: $60 (12 months avg retention)
- LTV:CAC = 30:1

---

## Success Criteria

**Go to Phase 2**:
- ✅ 100+ quizzes generated
- ✅ 40%+ quiz completion rate
- ✅ 20%+ repeat usage
- ✅ 4.0/5.0+ avg quality rating

**Pivot or Kill**:
- ❌ <50 quizzes in 4 weeks
- ❌ <20% completion rate
- ❌ <10% repeat usage
- ❌ <3.0/5.0 quality rating
